Verification

Check what you downloaded

# macOS
shasum -a 256 Vigrel-1.0.0-mac-arm64.dmg
# Linux
sha256sum Vigrel-1.0.0-linux-x86_64.AppImage
# Windows (PowerShell)
Get-FileHash Vigrel-1.0.0-setup-x64.exe -Algorithm SHA256

No build is code-signed with a commercial certificate yet. That is the next milestone; until then, verify the hash and the source.
