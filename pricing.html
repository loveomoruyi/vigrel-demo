Questions

Before you start

Why is there a subscription when the model is free?

Because the model is not the product. You are paying for the voice stack, the memory system, the interface, the skill packs, the updates and the support — the parts that turn a model into something you can use without a terminal. The weights stay free and can be replaced with your own.

What happens when the trial ends?

The app switches to read-only: memory, documents and exports remain available, while voice, builds and the large model pause. Nothing is deleted, and subscribing restores everything immediately.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Inference, speech and memory need no network. The licence is a signed file checked on the machine, with a 14-day offline grace period, so an air-gapped machine activates from a file.

What do you need from me?

An email and a payment method, for the licence only. No usage data, no telemetry, no conversation content, no analytics. The privacy page lists every byte that can leave the machine.

My machine is small. Do I still get Pro?

Yes. Pro detects what your hardware can run and uses the 35B locally where it fits; where it does not, it uses cloud burst for that turn. You never choose a model or a quantisation.

Can I cancel?

One click, in the app, with no retention flow. Your data exports whether you cancel or not, and the perpetual fallback still applies if you reach 24 months.
