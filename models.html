Specifications

Three models. One decision: what fits.

Vigrel picks its model from your hardware. Every figure here was measured on an M1 Max with 32 GB — load time, resident memory and tokens per second — not taken from a datasheet.

TierModelWeights on diskResident memorySpeed (measured)Model load
Core Qwen3-8B · Q4 5.0 GB 5.4 GB 43.2 tok/s 4 s
Pro Qwen2.5-14B-Instruct · Q4 9.0 GB 9.1 GB 24.9 tok/s 8 s
Studio Qwen3.6-35B-A3B · Q4 (mixture of experts) 22.4 GB 22.5 GB 52.5 tok/s 13 s

Speed measured with a 120-token completion at 4K context, one model resident, on an M1 Max 32 GB. The app picks the tier from installed memory — 16 GB gets the 8B, 24 GB the 14B, 32 GB and above the 35B — and a Core licence is limited to the first two. Dense models activate every parameter per token; the 35B activates 3B of 35B, which is why it is both the most capable and the fastest on this hardware.

Hardware requirements

What each tier needs

Unified memory on Apple Silicon, VRAM on Windows and Linux. Leave room for the operating system, the app and — on machines with voice enabled — the audio stack.

TierApple SiliconWindows / LinuxDisk for modelComfortable when
Core · 8B16 GB unified8 GB VRAM or 16 GB RAM (CPU)5 GB16 GB machine, voice only, light builds
Pro · 14B24 GB unified12 GB VRAM or 32 GB RAM (CPU)9 GB24 GB machine, documents and code
Studio · 35B36 GB unified or more24 GB VRAM23 GB36–64 GB machine, builds and vision
The 32 GB caveat, stated plainly. A 35B model at 22.5 GB on a 32 GB machine leaves too little headroom for the operating system and audio. It runs, and it is fast, but on a 32 GB Mac the honest configuration is Pro (14B), or Studio with cloud burst. Studio is specified at 36 GB and above for that reason, not to upsell.

Choosing

A two-line decision

If your machine has 16–24 GB

Choose Core or Pro. Voice, memory, documents and code all work. Builds are slower but real. Nothing is crippled, nothing is a trial.

If your machine has 36 GB or more

Choose Studio. The mixture-of-experts model is both faster and broader: agentic builds, vision, and long context without strain.

If your machine is thin, or the task is heavy

Choose the Cloud add-on. Compute runs remotely, the conversation and memory stay local, and nothing is stored server-side.

If you are air-gapped

Choose any local tier. All three run with no network path. The cloud add-on is simply not installed on those sites.
